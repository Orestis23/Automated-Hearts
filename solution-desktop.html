Negative-Software Solution

Consolidate

Bring critical information, tools, and handoffs into a smaller number of connected places. A reliable source of truth reduces hunting, duplicate entry, and conflicting versions, so every later step starts with cleaner context and fewer surprises.

55%of knowledge workers say information is hard to track down.Source: Atlassian, State of Teams 202450%have discovered another team was already working on the same project.Source: Atlassian, State of Teams 20244.9×more likely to be effective when teams make knowledge self-service.Source: Atlassian, State of Teams 2024

Streamline

Once the information is together, simplify the path the work follows. Remove unnecessary approvals, duplicate clicks, and unclear handoffs; define what happens next and who owns it. The result is a more predictable route from request to completion, with fewer places for work to stall.

58%of the workday was spent on “work about work” in Asana’s U.S. survey.Source: Asana, Anatomy of Work 20222 minaverage time between meetings, emails, or chat interruptions during core work hours.Source: Microsoft Work Trend Index 202525Bwork hours are lost to ineffective collaboration each year across the Fortune 500.Source: Atlassian, State of Teams 2024

Automate

Automate the stable, repeatable parts of that cleaner route—routing, reminders, updates, data entry, follow-up, and other routine actions. Keep people at the points where judgment matters while the system handles repetition consistently in the background.

41%of desk-worker time is spent on low-value, repetitive, or non-core work.Source: Slack Workforce Lab3.6 hrsper week saved on average by workers using automation tools.Source: Slack, State of Work62%of automation users report getting more done with fewer resources.Source: Slack, State of Work

Optimize

Use the visibility created by the connected system to see where work slows, fails, repeats, or creates unnecessary effort. Then tune timing, rules, exceptions, and workloads based on what actually happens, so the process gets more reliable instead of simply becoming more automated.

80%of business leaders say data is critical to decision-making.Source: Salesforce / YouGov 202341%say data is too complex or not accessible enough to understand.Source: Salesforce / YouGov 2023$5M+annual losses from poor data quality are estimated by more than 1 in 4 organizations.Source: IBM / Forrester 2025

Explore

Once the core workflow is stable, add capabilities that were impractical before: AI-assisted research, enrichment, prediction, personalization, new integrations, and new data sources. Experiments become safer and more useful because they plug into a system that already has structure.

75%of global knowledge workers reported using AI at work.Source: Microsoft + LinkedIn 202490%of AI users said AI helps them save time.Source: Microsoft + LinkedIn 202479%of leaders said AI adoption is critical to staying competitive.Source: Microsoft + LinkedIn 2024

Real-World Intelligence

Feed current internal and external information back into the workflow so the system can react to changing demand, schedules, inventory, customer behavior, market signals, and exceptions. Fresh context turns automation from a fixed sequence into a system that supports earlier, better-informed decisions.
