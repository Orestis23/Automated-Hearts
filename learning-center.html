Plain-English AI Glossary

Understand the Language. Keep Your Judgment.

Short, useful definitions for common AI, automation, data, and workflow terms.

⌕

20 terms shown

AI

Artificial intelligence is technology that performs tasks associated with human reasoning, pattern recognition, language, or decision support.

AI Agent

A system that can pursue a defined goal, use tools, and complete multiple steps with some level of independence.

Automation

A repeatable process completed by software or systems with less manual effort.

Automation with a Human Touch

The Automated Hearts approach: use technology to reduce friction while protecting human judgment, dignity, and connection.

Business Process

A repeatable series of steps used to complete a business outcome.

Chatbot

A conversational interface designed to answer questions or guide a user through a task.

Copilot

An AI assistant designed to work alongside a person inside a tool or workflow.

Dashboard

A visual view of important information, activity, or performance in one place.

Data

Recorded information that can be organized, compared, or used to support decisions.

Generative AI

AI that creates new text, images, audio, code, or other content from instructions and examples.

Hallucination

An AI response that sounds confident but contains invented, unsupported, or incorrect information.

Human-Centered Automation

Automation designed around the needs, judgment, safety, and experience of the people affected by it.

Input

The information, instruction, or data given to a system.

Integration

A connection that allows separate tools or systems to share information or trigger actions.

LLM

A large language model: an AI system trained to understand and generate language.

LLMO

Work intended to make information easier for large language models to understand, retrieve, and reference.

Process Mapping

Documenting the steps, decisions, handoffs, and information involved in completing work.

Prompt

The instruction, question, context, or example given to a generative AI system.

Structured Data

Information organized into predictable fields, rows, labels, or categories.

Workflow

The sequence of people, information, decisions, and actions used to complete work.
