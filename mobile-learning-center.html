AI 101

  • AI assists. People decide. Use artificial intelligence to support judgment, not replace responsibility.
  • Context creates clarity. Explain the goal, audience, source material, and desired format before asking for an answer.
  • Verify before you rely. Check important facts, dates, calculations, and claims before using AI-generated work.
  • Protect private information. Never enter confidential customer, employee, financial, or health information without an approved process.
  • The first answer is a draft. Question, refine, and improve the output instead of treating it as finished work.

Practical AI

  • AI Safety Basics. Know what information should stay out of public AI tools.
  • Better Prompts. Give useful context, define the task, and ask for a clear format.
  • Check the Answer. Verify facts and claims before relying on output.
  • Use Existing Tools. Look for AI features you already pay for.
  • Protect Human Judgment. Let AI support thinking, not replace responsibility.
  • Run Small Experiments. Test, measure, learn, and adapt.

Strategy Lab

  • Solve problems. Do not chase tools. Build AI strategy around a valuable business need, not the newest technology.
  • Clean data comes first. Organized, connected information creates more useful automation, reporting, and predictions.
  • Automate stable processes. Simplify and document a process before asking technology to repeat it.
  • Keep humans at consequence points. Require human review wherever decisions affect people, money, safety, or trust.
  • Scale proven systems. Expand only after a small system consistently produces measurable value.
