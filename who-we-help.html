Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers Before You Commit.

Start with understanding. No pressure. No forced overhaul.

What is human-centered automation?

It means improving work in a way that supports people, preserves judgment, and keeps human oversight where it matters.

Do we need to replace our current software?

Not necessarily. We begin by understanding what you already use and look for ways to get more value from it.

What kinds of work can be improved?

Common starting points include intake, scheduling, document flow, reporting, follow-ups, handoffs, and repeated data entry.

What if our team is not technical?

That is normal. Guidance is plain-English, paced around the team, and focused on confidence rather than technical performance.

Where should we start?

Start with one process that feels harder, slower, or more confusing than it should.
